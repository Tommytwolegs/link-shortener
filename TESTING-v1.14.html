Identity and first run

  1. Open chrome://extensions and find the extension card.
  2. Open the toolbar popup.

The card says Rather's Link Cleaner 1.14.0, and the popup header reads "Link Cleaner" with a v1.14.0 tag. Nothing anywhere still says Shortener.

  1. In chrome://extensions, REMOVE the unpacked extension, then Load unpacked again. (This counts as a fresh install. Your toggles live in sync storage and survive.)

A "Rather's Link Cleaner is ready" tab opens exactly once, with five cards and working Open settings / site / GitHub buttons. Reloading the extension later does NOT reopen it.

The new popup

  1. Open the popup and expand the Global group.

Every category (Search & productivity, Shopping, ...) is a collapsed dropdown row with a caret, an on-count like 11/11, and a small checkbox on the right. Opening one shows the per-site switches as before, gears included.

  1. Open the Event tickets category. Uncheck the master checkbox in its header.
  2. Open the dropdown: all four sites should be off. Turn ONE site back on.
  3. Look at the master checkbox again, then re-check it.

Unchecking turns all four rows off; with one row back on the master shows the mixed (dash) state and the count reads 1/4; re-checking the master turns all four on. Close and reopen the popup: the states stuck.

  1. In the popup, find the Airlines category (near Flights).

It lists 12 carriers (Delta ... Qatar Airways), each with its own switch, plus the master in the header. The old single "Airlines (12 carriers)" row is gone.

  1. Type delta in the popup's filter box.
  2. Clear it (Esc).

While filtering, the Delta row is visible (its category opened by itself); clearing folds the categories back up and restores your group arrangement.

  1. In the popup's Options dropdown, turn ON "Skip redirects before they load" (accept the permission prompt if asked).
