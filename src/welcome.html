Rather's Link Cleaner is ready

Messy links from all over the web, cleaned in place: your link stays your link, it just loses the tracking junk.

It's already working

Visit any of nearly 300 covered sites and the address bar cleans itself in place, after the page loads, so nothing ever breaks. The link you copy is the clean one.

Watch the badge

The counter on the toolbar icon shows how much was cleaned on the page you are on. Click it to see exactly which parameters were removed, and to copy the clean link in any format.

Clean any link, anywhere

Right-click any link and choose "Copy clean URL". It works on every site, sees through redirect wrappers, and there is a keyboard shortcut, an address-bar keyword, and a bulk cleaner for whole emails too.

Three deeper modes, when you want them

In the popup's Options: clean every site you visit, block trackers before the request even leaves your browser, and skip tracker redirect pages entirely. All off by default; your browser asks for the extra permission only when you turn one on, and it is handed back when they are all off.

Zero data collection

No analytics, no telemetry, no network requests of any kind. Your settings are the only thing stored. Open source, so you can verify every word of that.
